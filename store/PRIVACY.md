# Skip the Critics — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 3, 2026_

Skip the Critics does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.

**What the extension does**
- On Google search result pages, it looks for links to Rotten Tomatoes movie or TV pages.
- For each one, it loads that public Rotten Tomatoes page directly from your browser to read the audience score (Popcornmeter), without sending cookies.
- It saves those scores on your own device for up to 12 hours, so pages load faster. This data never leaves your browser.

**What it does not do**
- It does not read, record, or transmit your search queries or browsing history.
- It has no analytics, tracking, ads, or remote servers of its own.
- It does not send any data to the developer or any third party.

Skip the Critics is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rotten Tomatoes, Fandango, or Google.

Questions: contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
